Farm infrastructure in GrainDash imagery must be credible for the farm's scale. The user explained that a farm as small as the one in the original hero example would not have a grain-leg setup.

**Why:** The user identified the mismatch and asked for a more believable farm background.

**How to apply:** Review generated farm scenes for equipment plausibility, not just visual quality. For modest prairie farm scenes, avoid grain legs and industrial terminal infrastructure.

Preserve the lighter farm-first direction rather than returning to the live site's darker background.

**Why:** The user specifically liked its lighter background and called the warm prairie panorama with restored large devices “almost perfect.”

**How to apply:** Keep the natural, warm landscape and realistic farm setting when refining nearby hero elements. Do not reinterpret small control-size changes as permission to redesign the background.
