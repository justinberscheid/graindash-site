---
name: Authentic product screens
description: Preserve genuine GrainDash UI when improving marketing device renders.
---

Premium marketing treatments can change device frames, materials, lighting, shadows, and composition, but must preserve the genuine product-screen content.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested better-looking products while saying the UI contents must not turn into gibberish.

**How to apply:** Use original screenshot pixels or faithful crops inside newly constructed hardware. Do not use AI-generated replacements for dashboards, labels, charts, or numbers. Judge legibility as well as the quality of the device surrounding the screen.
